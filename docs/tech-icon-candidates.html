Technology Icons

Icons for the 56 technologies, three choices each, grouped by era. Each is shown the way the game will show it: on its tech tree card, big in the research picker, in the top bar's research chip, and in the “You learned …” message. They'll also go on the Almanac cards and the leader cards' starting tech. None of them is an icon the game already uses for a unit, building, wonder, resource, or map mark. Until you pick, the game shows no tech icons, as now.

Tap one icon per technology to pick it (tap again to undo). Your picks are remembered on this device. When you're done, tap Copy my picks (or Share), or use the short form in Dan's picks at the bottom (“all A except …”), and give it to the planning session. The picks get wired in the round after.
