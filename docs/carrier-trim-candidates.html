Battleship vs Carrier at map size

Your picks were Battleship A and Carrier A, which look alike when small. The trimmed Carrier drops the wave lines and is cropped a little larger, so it reads as one flat deck with a plane, while the Battleship keeps its three waves. The discs are the real map sizes (31 px open field, 22 px in a city). Icons by Cathelineau, game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0 (the Carrier modified for Epoch).
