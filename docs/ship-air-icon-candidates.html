Ship and Aircraft Icons

Tap one icon per unit to pick it (tap again to undo). Your picks are remembered on this device, separately from the land-unit page. When you’re done, tap Copy my picks (or Share) in the bar at the bottom and paste the list into the chat. Each candidate is shown large, then at real map size: the 31 px disc used in the open field (blue and purple civs) and the 22 px disc used inside a city. The Watch out note under each unit points out icons that look alike. The overview at the bottom shows every candidate side by side, next to the 15 land icons already in the game. Icons are from game-icons.net under CC BY 3.0; the author of each icon you pick will be credited in the game.

Ships (next round)

Ships are shown on an ocean tile, where they’ll sit on the map.

Galley

Watch out: A (Galley) has three masts with crosses and can pass for a sailing warship; check it against Frigate A (Galleon) and Caravel A at the small size.

Caravel

Watch out: B (Sailboat) and C (Small Fishing Sailboat) look a lot like Frigate B (Schooner). C and Frigate B are by the same author and drawn the same way.

Frigate

Watch out: Only two good matches. A (Galleon) is a three-masted ship like Galley A and Caravel A, so pick one that differs from those two.

Ironclad

Watch out: Only two good matches. A (Iron Hulled Warship) has a smoke plume like Battleship B (Dreadnought) and comes from the same set, so don't pick both.

Transport

Watch out: Only two good matches. A (Cargo Ship) has a stack of crates and is easy to spot. B (Ship Bow) has a small anchor on the hull.

Destroyer

Watch out: A (Cruiser) is from the same set as Battleship A and Carrier A (a ship over wave lines). The three are nearly identical at 22 px, so pick at most one of them. C (Propeller) is a symbol rather than a ship, but it's the clearest at map size.

Battleship

Watch out: Only two good matches. A looks like Destroyer A and Carrier A. B (Dreadnought) has a smoke plume like Ironclad A.

Submarine

Watch out: A (Submarine) is a flat cigar shape, like Fighter A (Airplane) at 22 px. C is a missile leaving the water, so it may read as a rocket.

Carrier

Watch out: Only one real aircraft carrier icon exists. B (Airplane Departure) is a plane taking off over a deck line and has the same airliner shape as Bomber A. A looks like Destroyer A and Battleship A; its small plane is the only difference.

Aircraft (round 10)

Aircraft arrive in round 10. You can pick them now; the picks will be kept. They’re shown over grassland and over ocean.

Fighter

Watch out: A (Airplane) is a thin side view that looks like Submarine A at 22 px. B (Biplane) is a front view and gets busy when small. C (Air Force) is a winged badge, not a plane.

Bomber

Watch out: A (Commercial Airplane) has the same shape as Carrier B. B (Carpet Bombing) has small bomb dots that disappear at 22 px. C is a bomb, not a plane.

Jet Fighter

Watch out: Only two good matches. B (Starfighter) is a sci-fi craft seen from above, similar in style to Stealth Bomber C (Scout Ship).

Stealth Bomber

Watch out: A is a solid triangle, very clear. B (Paper Plane) is also a wedge and could pass for a jet. C (Scout Ship) is sci-fi.

Helicopter

Watch out: Only two good matches. B (Dragonfly) stands in for a helicopter. It's bold and distinct, but it's an insect.
