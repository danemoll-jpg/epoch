When you’re done, tap Copy my picks (or Share) in the bar at the bottom and paste the list into the chat. Each candidate is shown large, then at real map size on the terrain it will sit on. Map features

The barbarian village (with 0–4 flags), the exploration hut, and the small badge that marks a barbarian unit. Village and hut icons are drawn dark on a pale backing (square for the village, round for the hut) so they stand out on any terrain; the final styling can change.

Village

Watch out: B (Wooden fort) is a row of thin stakes; it stays readable at 30 px but loses detail on forest. C (Tipi) is a tent like Hut A’s hut, so don’t pick both.

Hut

Watch out: B (Ancient ruins) is a busy drawing and gets muddy at 24 px. C (Stepped pyramid) may later be confused with a wonder.

Barbarian badge

Watch out: At 14 px only the rough outline survives. A (Skull) reads best. C (Horned helmet) is a helmet like the Centurion Helmet land-unit icon already in the game.

Resources

Each resource sits in the tile’s upper-right corner at 18 px. Each tile pair shows the two styles: white on a dark badge and plain color, on the terrain(s) where it appears.

Wheat

Watch out: B (Grain sack) turns into a lumpy blob at 18 px.

Cattle

Watch out: B (Bull head) is mostly thin lines and nearly vanishes at 18 px. A (Cow) has spots that disappear; it still reads as an animal.

Game

Watch out: A (Deer) and Cattle C (Bison) are both animals side-on; check them together in the overview.

Fish

Watch out: C (Fish bone) reads as leftovers rather than a fishing ground.

Whales

Watch out: A (Whale) includes a small palm island that becomes noise at 18 px, and the palm is close to Oasis B.

Oasis

Watch out: Desert is a pale tile, so the plain-color version needs a dark color (shown in green). The badge version reads better here.

Spices

Watch out: A (Spice heap) has fine sparks that disappear at 18 px and could pass for a fire.

Silk

Watch out: Weak options: game-icons.net has no silkworm or silk icon, so all three are cloth stand-ins.

Wine

Watch out: B (Bottle and glass) is two thin shapes and is hard to make out at 18 px.

Gold

Watch out: B (Gold bar) has the same shape as Aluminum B (Metal bar); pick at most one of them. A (Nuggets) looks like Iron B (Ore) at 18 px.

Gems

Watch out: A (Diamond) is the clearest at map size. C (Crystal) is thin and spiky.

Iron

Watch out: B (Ore) looks like Gold A (Nuggets) at 18 px. C (Pickaxe) could stand for any mine.

Aluminum

Watch out: Weak options: there is no aluminum icon, so these are stand-ins. B (Metal bar) matches Gold B in shape. C (Metal sheet) reads as a plain square at 18 px.

Rubber

Watch out: Weak options: no rubber tree exists, so these are stand-ins for “rubber”. A (Tire) is a ring like Great Engineer A’s gear at small size.

Oil

Watch out: A (Pump jack) is thin-lined and muddy at 18 px. C (Drop) is the clearest, but could be read as water.

Great People

Shown at 40 px in a panel and at 22 px on a gold disc on the map.

Great Scientist

Watch out: B (Atom) is thin orbits and gets faint on the 22 px disc.

Great Artist

Watch out: C (Theater masks) is two faces overlapping and gets muddy on the 22 px disc.

Great Merchant

Watch out: A (Coins) is close to Gold (a resource) in meaning; keep them apart if you pick both.

Great Engineer

Watch out: A (Gears) and B (Hammer in gear) are both round gears; Rubber A (Tire) is also a ring.

Great General

Watch out: B (Crossed swords) is close to Barbarian badge B (Crossed axes); don’t pick both.
