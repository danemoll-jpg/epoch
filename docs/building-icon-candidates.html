Building Icons

Icons for the 17 buildings and the wonders. They'll show in the city panel's build list (the dark row, at 28 px), next to a building's name where the city lists what it has (the small chip), and on the Almanac cards. Until you pick, the game shows no icon there, as now. For a wonder you can pick its own icon, or leave it and it gets the generic wonder icon.

Tap one icon per subject to pick it (tap again to undo). Your picks are remembered on this device. When you're done, tap Copy my picks (or Share) and paste the list into the chat. The picks get wired in the round after.
