# Religion icon candidates: sources

All icons are from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) (SVG sources:
https://github.com/game-icons/icons), licensed **CC BY 3.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Whichever icons are used must
credit their authors. The files here are normalized: `viewBox="0 0 512 512"`,
the site's black background square removed, the shape's path(s) with
`fill="currentColor"`. The shapes themselves are unchanged.

These are for Round 12's religion: the Missionary unit, one symbol per founded
religion (8 subjects), and the holy city marker. No real-world religious
symbols are offered, on purpose. The Mountain subject stands in for a Moon,
because every moon icon on game-icons.net is a crescent.

The picker page is `docs/religion-road-icon-candidates.html` (picks are saved
on the device under `epoch.religionIconPicks`).
