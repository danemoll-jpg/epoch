Terrain and city styles

Every picture on this page is drawn by the game's own code, on a small made-up map with every terrain, a coastline and an island, two nations' cities (one walled, a capital, a holy city), units on land and sea, resources, a hut, a barbarian village, a road and a railroad, and fog (the dim tiles are explored but out of sight). Pick one terrain style (A, B, or C) and one city style (A or B); they don't have to match. "Now" is today's look, for comparison. The bottom of the page shows your two picks together. A city's look follows its size (village, town, city, metropolis) and its owner's era (rows). Walls are drawn when a city has them. The capital star, religion, and holy-city badges stay as they are, drawn over the city.
