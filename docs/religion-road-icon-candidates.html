Religion Icons

These are for Round 12’s religion: the Missionary unit, the symbol each founded religion gets, and the marker on a religion’s holy city. Until you pick, the game shows the letters “Mi” for the Missionary and plain colored dots for religions; your picks get wired in next round. No real-world religious symbols are offered, on purpose (no crosses, crescents, six-point stars, wheels, and so on): every symbol here is a generic shape, so the religions you name stay your own.

Tap one icon per subject to pick it (tap again to undo). Your picks are remembered on this device, separately from the other icon pages. When you’re done, tap Copy my picks (or Share) in the bar at the bottom and paste the list into the chat. Each candidate is shown large, then at real size: the Missionary white on its owner’s color like the other units, each religion symbol white on that religion’s color in a city’s bottom-left corner, and the holy city marker in the bottom-right corner. The Watch out notes point out icons that look alike or get muddy when small. Icons are from game-icons.net under CC BY 3.0; the author of each icon you pick will be credited in the game.

Missionary

A unit, drawn like the others: white on its owner’s color on the map (20 px), and white at 40 px in the panels.

Missionary

Watch out: A (Hooded traveler) is mid-stride and reads as “on the move”, but it could also pass for a scout or an assassin. C (Hooded face) is only a head and gets close to a plain blob at 20 px.

Religion symbols

Each founded religion gets one of these eight symbols, drawn white on the religion’s color: at 12 px in a small disc in the bottom-left corner of each city that follows it, and at 22 px on a label in the panels. The religion names on the labels are only examples; you’ll name your own.

Sun disc

Watch out: B’s face disappears at 12 px and it becomes a spiky disc. Both suns have rays like Eye B and Holy city B; check them together in the overview.

Flame

Watch out: B (Three flames) is round teardrops and could be read as water drops at 12 px.

Star

Watch out: A is the same shape as the small star the game already draws in a capital’s top-left corner. B (Four-point star) looks like Holy city A (Sparkles); don’t pick both.

Eye

Watch out: B has rays around it and can look like a sun at 12 px. A’s thin outline gets faint at 12 px.

Tree

Watch out: A (Oak) turns into a round blob on a trunk at 12 px. B (Pine) keeps the crisper outline.

Spiral

Watch out: A is fine lines and is the one most likely to go faint at 12 px. B (Vortex) is bolder but reads more like a pinwheel.

Mountain

Watch out: This stands in for the Moon: every moon icon on game-icons.net is a crescent, and crescents were on the list to avoid. A’s ring makes it a disc inside a disc at 12 px.

Wave

Watch out: B (Wavy lines) is three thin stripes that blur together at 12 px. A is the clearer shape.

Holy city

A small gold-on-dark badge in the bottom-right corner of the city where a religion was founded (next to its religion disc), and at 40 px in the city panel.

Holy city

Watch out: A (Sparkles) is the same idea as Star B (Four-point star); don’t pick both. B (Pillar of light) has rays like the Sun disc icons. C has a lot of fine rays that vanish at 12 px.
